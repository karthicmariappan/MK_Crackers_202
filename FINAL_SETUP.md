# MK Crackers — Browse + Cart + Download Price List

- Right-side floating Price List panel: Download Excel + Download PDF.
- Complete supplied price list is browsable inside the website.
- Search products and filter categories.
- Enter quantity for each cracker; line amount and total amount calculate automatically.
- Add products to the enquiry cart.
- Cart shows total quantity and total amount.
- Customer enters name, mobile, WhatsApp, location, pincode, preferred contact and notes.
- Submit enquiry sends data to your Google Apps Script / Google Sheet.
- You call the customer back to confirm availability and fulfilment.

Before publishing:
1. Open `public/app.js`.
2. Replace `PASTE_GOOGLE_APPS_SCRIPT_WEB_APP_URL_HERE` with your current Google Apps Script `/exec` URL.
3. Upload every file inside `public/` to GitHub Pages.
4. Keep `MK-Crackers-Price-List.xlsx` and `MK-Crackers-Price-List.pdf` in the same public folder.
